I entered university in 2018 and started working right away: social media, then branding, until I found UX/UI. During the pandemic I specialized in e-commerce UI and, since then, creating websites and prototypes has been the core of my work. I realized the best projects were beautiful and also sold.

That is why I care as much about clarity, conversion and consistency as I do about aesthetics. A brand runs through the name, the logo, social media, the product, all the way to the website checkout. Today I work remotely, from Curitiba, for Bananas Global.
