Preventive and strategic tax law.

Visual identity for Frias Constantino Advocacia, a tax law firm in Tatuapé, São Paulo. An owl in an engraving line, an FC monogram of overlapping initials and a palette of green, sand and gold communicate precision before the very first conversation.

A brand built with the same rigor it stands for.

Concept

Every choice, from the line to the typography, answers to an intention: to communicate precision before the very first conversation.

The owl

The owl crosses cultures as a symbol of wisdom. It is the companion of Athena, the Greek goddess of strategy. But what makes it right for this brand is its behavior: it sees where others do not, flies in absolute silence and acts with surgical precision. Observation, discretion and precision: the same triad that defines good tax law. Drawn in an engraving line, it recalls the legal and editorial tradition; in flight, it conveys a practice that does not wait for the problem: it anticipates.

Monogram

The FC monogram is born from the overlap of the initials, not side by side, but crossed. The F rests on the structure of the C, and the C embraces the stem of the F. It is a construction that only works because the two shapes were drawn for each other, just as a good argument only holds when each point ties into the next. The design comes from the tradition of seal monograms: the stamp that, long before digital signatures, validated documents and vouched for what was written. On its own, the monogram is the most condensed form of the brand: where the full name does not fit, it answers.

Colors

The palette starts from green, the color of stability, balance and prosperity, in a family that goes from deep to serene, holding the brand with the same constancy expected from good counsel. Over it, sand and browns bring the warmth of paper and leather, a repertoire inherited from the world of documents and legal libraries. Gold, always in a precise dose, is the point of value: it appears where the brand wants to lead the eye, like a thread of emphasis amid sobriety.

Applications

The identity takes shape on paper, fabric and screen: gold in relief over deep green, the owl as a watermark and the monogram as a signature, where the full name does not fit.

Precision before the first conversation.
